# 2×2 Rubik scene — prototype, 2026-10-13

Controller positions/orientations drive the wrists; ordinary independent Grip
closes the existing G1 fingers. The object has eight physical colored cubies,
a free core, passive layer hinges and a magnetic torque inside 15° of a quarter
revolution. There are no hand/world grasp attachments or face motors in the
runtime model. Its switchable internal retaining bindings are idealized.

## Verified

Free G1 hand-contact lift: cube center 0.934 → 1.001449 m, +67.449 mm; loaded
hand contacts for 3119 of 3500 steps. Author 20k at 100 Hz, physics 1000 Hz,
runtime-equivalent direct arm PD and ordinary Grip. Robot root remained free;
minimum pelvis height 0.783994 m, zero MuJoCo warnings. Only the initial arm
pose was set as a fixture; during measurement no cube/robot coordinates were
rewritten. Wrist IK for this offline probe is independent of live GMR.

An externally loaded mechanism fixture rotated the four real corner bodies to
90.0222°. The core was fixed in this test. It proves passive physical face
motion, not G1 bimanual manipulation. Magnet: 12° → 0.000203°; at 25° the face
stayed outside the capture window. Snapshot restoration, no coordinate or
velocity overwrite at a quarter-turn, and inverse-scramble color recognition
passed native checks.

Controller mapping, independent wrists, orientation axes, tracking loss and
calibration using current FK passed. 91 unit tests: 90 passed, 1 optional Torch
skipped. All 21 synthetic replay cases had zero state/reference error.

APK ZIP CRC, packaged model/source hashes and signing verification passed.
Visual budget: 96697 triangles per eye including HUD/skeleton reserve. Preview
uses the native shaders on desktop; it is not a headset screenshot.

## Not accepted

Reliable controlled bimanual quarter-turn through G1 hands and full manual VR
solve are not demonstrated. Hand probes lose contacts, select another axis or
drop the cube. Raw turn counts, unplanned turns, and turns after falling do not
count as success. The 94.8 mm experiment did not improve grasp and was reverted.
The final scene is 62.8 mm / 122 g. Quest FPS and usability are unverified.

The APK is a prototype and has not been installed. Unrelated existing local
changes were preserved; nothing was committed or pushed.

Evidence: `summary.json`, `apk-verification.json`, `source-verification.json`,
`final-tests.log`, `final-replay.log`, `loaded-face-test.log`,
`grasp-final63/result.json`, `grasp-final63/grasp-trace.npz`. Other probe outputs
are historical failed/diagnostic experiments and are not acceptance evidence.
