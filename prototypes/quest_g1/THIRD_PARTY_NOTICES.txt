G1 geometry under assets/g1 is adapted from Unitree Robotics meshes and
MuJoCo Menagerie's MJX collision model. The original articulated mesh files
are also distributed by the pinned TWIST2 checkout above.
Menagerie revision: f054586a8e90465d49ee5be15335c4a0c7f57caf
https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/f054586a8e90465d49ee5be15335c4a0c7f57caf/unitree_g1

The full Unitree BSD-3-Clause notice is retained in assets/g1/LICENSE and
packaged as G1_GEOMETRY_LICENSE.txt in Android assets. Modifications include
exterior-shell remeshing, topology reduction, crease-aware normals and
per-link convex hand proxies; no upstream endorsement is implied.
